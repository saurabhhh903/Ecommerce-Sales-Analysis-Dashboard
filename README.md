#  Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** built to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, stores, regions, and returns.

The project focuses on converting raw ecommerce data into meaningful **business insights and actionable recommendations**.

---

## Dashboard Preview

![Ecommerce Sales Dashboard](overview.png)

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to answer real-world business questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit is the business generating?
- Which products and brands perform best?
- Which stores and regions generate the highest revenue?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which brands have stronger profit margins?
- What is the overall return rate?
- Which products and store types have higher return activity?
- What business actions can be taken from the analysis?

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance.

**Key KPIs:**

- Total Transactions
- Total Revenue
- Total Quantity
- Total Cost
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Return Rate

### 2. Product & Profit Analysis

Analyzes product and brand-level sales and profitability.

**Key Visuals:**

- Profit Margin by Brand
- Top 5 Brands by Revenue
- Top Products by Profit
- Top Products by Revenue

![Product & Profit Analysis](Dashboard%20page/product%20and%20profit.jpg)

### 3. Customer Analysis

Analyzes customer segments and purchasing behavior.

**Key Analysis:**

- Sales by Member Card

- Customer-level performance

![Customer Analysis](Dashboard%20page/Customerse.jpg)

### 4. Store & Regional Analysis

### 5. Returns & Regional Analysis
